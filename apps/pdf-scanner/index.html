🚀 All-in-One PDF Scanner App

PDF Scanner is designed to make document management simple and efficient. Whether you are a student, professional, or business user, this app provides everything you need to scan, edit, and share documents instantly. Turn your smartphone into a powerful Document Scanner & OCR tool. Easily scan documents, convert images to PDF, edit files, and manage everything in one place — fast, secure, and completely offline.

📸 Scan Documents with High Quality

Scan any document using your camera and convert it into a clean, professional PDF effortlessly.

  • Scan anything - Documents, receipts, and notes
  • ID Cards & Passports - Perfectly align and format IDs
  • Auto edge detection & cropping - Fast and accurate alignment
  • Enhance image quality - Automatically adjust brightness and contrast
  • Multi-page scanning support - Batch scan capabilities

Create perfect digital copies in seconds.

🔍 OCR – Extract Text from Images

Use powerful OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to translate images into actionable text.

  • Extract text from scanned documents - Quick digitizing
  • Copy and share text easily - Immediate access
  • Convert images into readable content - Editable text versions

Perfect for students, office work, and quick notes.

✏️ Edit PDF Easily & Convert Instantly

Edit PDF

  • ✔ Add text to PDF
  • ✔ Add signature
  • ✔ Reorder, rotate, and delete pages
  • ✔ Annotate documents directly

Convert Files

  • ✔ Image to PDF
  • ✔ PDF to Image
  • ✔ PDF to Long Image
  • ✔ Fast and high-quality conversion

Make changes quickly and professionally, no internet required.

📦 Powerful PDF Tools & Security

Manage Documents

Merge files, split pages, compress size, and add watermarks effortlessly. Organize documents easily in one app.

🔐 Secure Your Documents

Lock PDF with password, unlock PDFs securely. Keep your private files safe knowing your data stays secure and private.
